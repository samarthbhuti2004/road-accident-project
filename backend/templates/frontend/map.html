🚦 Road Accident Risk Map

Click a city marker to see its accident statistics, why it has that risk level, and its main hazard.

How risk level is decided
Cities are ranked by the share of their accidents that were major or fatal (serious accidents). The top third is High, the middle third Medium, the bottom third Low.
Note: the differences between cities in this dataset are small (a few percentage points), so the levels show relative ranking, not a big gap in danger.
